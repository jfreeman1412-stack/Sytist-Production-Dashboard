TWO FIXES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1) REAL COMPOSITE THUMBNAIL FOR GREEN-SCREEN ITEMS

   Phase 12a put the background photo as a small corner inset
   because I assumed we'd need server-side chroma-keying to
   really composite a green-screen photo. Wrong assumption —
   the player photos in this pipeline are already PNGs with
   the green keyed out (transparent background). So a real
   composite is trivial: layer the background photo
   underneath the player PNG, browser handles the alpha.

   Now the green-screen thumbnail shows what the actual
   render will look like:

     - Background photo fills the 80×80 tile (objectFit:cover)
     - Player PNG layers on top (objectFit:cover, alpha shows
       background through transparent regions)
     - Player's click-through opens the player photo
       full-size; tooltip names the chosen background

   For non-green-screen items, only the player photo renders
   (unchanged).

2) CLEARER PACKING SLIP PREVIEW MESSAGE

   The Darkroom TXT preview was saying:

     Packing slip: not included (slip path not given)

   ...which made it sound like the slip was misconfigured or
   missing. The truth: this is a PREVIEW of the .txt without
   the slip line, because slip generation only happens at
   actual processing time. The slip IS included in the .txt
   that gets written to disk when you click Process.

   New text:

     Packing slip: generated at processing time
                   (not shown in this preview)

   Same meaning, but doesn't sound like an error.
