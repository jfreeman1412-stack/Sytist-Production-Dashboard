Phase 11c — auto-lock graphics + click-through locked slots

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE PROBLEM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Even with the layers panel from Phase 11b, you couldn't actually
DRAG the player photo. Selecting "Player photo" from the layers
panel worked, but as soon as you clicked the canvas to start
dragging, the click hit the static graphic on top and re-selected
that instead. You were back to square one — the topmost slot
intercepting your interaction.

Root cause: in the canvas, locked slots blocked clicks (calling
stopPropagation), but they also didn't let those clicks fall
through to the layer beneath. They were a dead zone.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE FIX (two parts)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PART 1: Canvas — locked slots are click-through

  Added `pointer-events: none` on the SVG group when a slot is
  locked. The browser routes clicks past the locked slot to
  whatever is underneath. The locked slot still renders visually
  (so the operator sees it as context) but no longer intercepts
  mouse events.

  This also applies in the layout designer — locked slots there
  used to be dead zones too. Click-through is the right behavior
  in both places: "locked" means "protected from edits," which
  by extension means "shouldn't block edits to other slots."

PART 2: Override editor — auto-lock graphics + backgrounds + a
        per-layer lock toggle

  When a layout loads in the override editor, slots of kind
  `staticGraphic`, `overlay`, and `playerBackground` are
  automatically locked. These are typically background elements
  that don't need per-order adjustment.

  Each row in the layers panel now has a 🔒/🔓 icon at the right.
  Click to toggle. Unlock a layer to edit it; lock it again to
  protect it.

  Locked layers show in the panel with reduced opacity so the
  operator can see at a glance which layers are protected.

  If you select a locked layer (from the panel or anywhere else),
  the "Selected slot" section shows "🔒 This layer is locked —
  click the 🔒 icon in the layers list to unlock" instead of the
  editable property fields. Prevents accidental edits via the
  number inputs.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WORKFLOW NOW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Open an override editor that has a graphic over the player photo:

  Layers (click any row to select)
  ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
  │ ▣ Static graphic           🔒  │  ← auto-locked
  │ ▣ Player photo             🔓  │  ← editable
  │ ▣ Player background        🔒  │  ← auto-locked
  └─────────────────────────────────┘

You click the player photo area on the canvas → selects player
photo (graphic is click-through). Drag → player moves. Wheel
scrolls → player scales.

If you decide you DO need to adjust the graphic position for
this one order, click its 🔒 icon. It becomes 🔓. Now canvas
clicks land on it and you can drag/resize/scale as normal. Lock
it again afterward to keep it from getting in the way.

HOW TO VERIFY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Open an override editor that has a static graphic over the
     player photo.

     The player photo (underneath) selects, not the graphic.

     scales it.

     — it becomes 🔓. Now you can click and drag the graphic too.
  6. Click 🔓 to re-lock when done.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ALSO AFFECTS THE LAYOUT DESIGNER (heads-up)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The click-through behavior for locked slots is in the shared
LayoutCanvas, so it also applies in the layout designer. If you
were used to clicks "absorbing" into locked slots in the
designer, they'll now pass through to whatever's underneath.

This is the right behavior (and what most graphics tools do —
locked layers are click-through), but flagging it because it's
a subtle change to existing functionality you've been using.
