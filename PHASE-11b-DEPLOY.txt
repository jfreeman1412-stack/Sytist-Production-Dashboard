Phase 11b — layers panel in the override editor

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE PROBLEM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

In the override editor canvas, clicking on the player or team
photo was selecting the static graphic on top of them instead.
There was no other way to select a slot, so the player/team
slots were effectively un-editable when a graphic overlay
existed.

This was because static graphics are rendered last in the layer
order (so they appear on top in the composite), which means they
also sit on top in the SVG and intercept all mouse clicks for
slots beneath them.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE FIX
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Added a Layers panel to the editor's right sidebar, above
"Selected slot."

  - One row per slot
  - Shows the slot kind (Player photo, Static graphic, Logo, etc.)
    with a colored swatch matching the canvas swatches
  - Text slots also show their text content for distinguishing
    multiple text layers
  - REVERSE display order — top of the visual stack appears first
    in the list (same convention as the layout designer's panel)
  - Click any row to select that layer. Selecting from the panel
    works even when the slot is fully obscured on canvas.

Canvas click-to-select still works for unobstructed slots — the
layers panel is the fallback when clicking is blocked by an
overlapping slot.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHY NOT ALSO MAKE GRAPHICS NON-CLICKABLE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

I considered making static graphics ignore canvas clicks so
that clicking through a graphic always lands on the layer
underneath. It'd be nicer for direct manipulation. But:

  - It complicates the canvas (the override editor would need
    its own variant of LayoutCanvas behavior)
  - The layers panel solves the immediate problem cleanly
  - If you find graphics still get in the way during real use,
    I can add the click-through behavior as a follow-up

HOW TO VERIFY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Open an override that has a static graphic over the player
   photo
2. Look at the right sidebar — Layers section appears above
   Selected slot
3. Click "Player photo" in the layers list — it selects, the
   quick-edit panel populates with x/y/w/h, the canvas shows
   resize handles around the player photo slot
4. Click "Static graphic" → graphic is selected instead
5. Edit values, scale with mouse wheel, drag — all works as
   before, just with reliable selection
