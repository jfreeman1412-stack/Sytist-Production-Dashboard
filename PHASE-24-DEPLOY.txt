Phase 24 — Bigger designer, bigger bleed area, narrower right panel,
            bigger settings text

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT THIS DOES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. DESIGNER CANVAS BIGGER
   TARGET_LONG_SIDE bumped from 700 → 950 pixels in LayoutDesignerPage.
   The canvas takes more horizontal space on the page.

2. BLEED AREA BIGGER
   In LayoutCanvas, the viewBox padding around the canvas bumped
   from 30% → 60% of the smaller canvas dimension. For a 6×4 canvas
   that's 2.4″ of bleed on each side instead of 1.2″.

3. RIGHT PANEL NARROWER
   Grid columns changed from `${canvasWidth + 80}px 1fr` to `3fr 1fr`.
   Now the right column takes ~25% of the row (was: everything left
   over after a fixed-width canvas column).

4. SETTINGS TEXT BIGGER
   In SettingsForm.js — used by EVERY settings page in the app:
     - FormRow label:      12 → 13
     - FormRow hint:       11 → 12
     - Input fontSize:     13 → 14  (TextInput, NumberInput, Select, TextArea)
     - Button fontSize:    12 → 13
     - settingsStyles.table fontSize: 12 → 13

   This affects all of /settings/* (Paths, Add-ons, Packages,
   Specialty, Composite, Imposition, etc.) — not just the designer.
