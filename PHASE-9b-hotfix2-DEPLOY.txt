═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT THIS HOTFIX ADDRESSES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ISSUE 1 — "Add" buttons disappeared after creating first layer.

  Diagnosis: the Add buttons were in the panel header at the top of
  the layers list, but when a slot was selected its card body
  expanded inline showing the full property editor. On a normal
  monitor, the panel got taller than the viewport — the Add buttons
  scrolled out of view.

  Fix: panel header is now `position: sticky` with `top: 0`. The
  Add buttons stay visible at the top of the viewport as you scroll
  through layer cards, so adding new layers is always one click
  away regardless of how many layers you have or which is expanded.

ISSUE 2 — No drag-and-drop for layer reorder.

  Each layer card now has a ⋮⋮ drag handle on the left. Click and
  drag the handle to reorder. As you drag over other cards, a blue
  bar appears either above or below the target card showing where
  the dragged card will land (depends on cursor position relative
  to the target's midpoint).

  The ↑/↓ buttons are still there as a parallel option, per your
  preference.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOW TO TRY IT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STICKY ADD BUTTONS:
  1. Open any layout in the designer
  2. Click a layer card to expand it (full property editor appears)
  3. Scroll down within the panel — the "+ Player / + Team / etc."
     buttons stay at the top of the viewport
  4. Click any of them to add a new layer at any time

DRAG TO REORDER:
  1. Hover over the ⋮⋮ icon on the left of any layer card —
     cursor becomes "grab"
  2. Click and hold the ⋮⋮, drag up or down
  3. Move over another card — a blue bar appears either above or
     below it showing where your card will land
  4. Release to drop. The layer order updates immediately.
  5. Save when done.

  The drag rules:
    - Cursor in TOP HALF of target card = drop ABOVE that card
      (in panel, which means dragged card lands ON TOP of target
      in canvas)
    - Cursor in BOTTOM HALF = drop BELOW
    - Drop on yourself = no-op
    - Drop where you already are = no-op

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SAFETY NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- The drag handle (⋮⋮) is the ONLY way to initiate a drag. Clicking
  anywhere else on the card still selects/expands it. This prevents
  accidental drags when you're trying to click to select.

- During a drag, the dragged card becomes 40% opacity so you can
  visually track it.

- The ↑/↓ buttons still work the same as before. They were not
  removed — just supplemented with drag.

- All reorder operations (drag OR ↑/↓) are part of the unsaved-
  changes state. They don't auto-save. The "● Unsaved changes"
  indicator at the top shows when any reorder needs saving.

- Discard reverts ALL changes since last save, including reorders.

- Drag works in modern Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari. Native HTML5
  drag-and-drop, no extra dependencies.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
KNOWN GAPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- DRAG OUTSIDE THE PANEL: dragging a layer card OUT of the layers
  panel does nothing. The drop will just be canceled. Future
  enhancement could allow dragging onto the canvas to position
  the slot — out of scope for now.

- DRAG TO ADD: you can't drag a slot kind from the Add buttons
  toolbar onto the canvas to position it directly. Adding still
  drops at the centered default position; you drag from there.

- TOUCH/MOBILE: HTML5 drag-and-drop has poor mobile support.
  This designer is desktop-only by design (1500×2100 layouts
  don't make sense on a phone). Not adding touch handlers.
