Phase 12a — background photo inset on green-screen line items

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE PROBLEM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Order detail page thumbnails for green-screen line items
showed only the raw player photo (with the green still in
the background). Operator couldn't tell what background the
customer had chosen — that info was hiding in the underlying
data but never surfaced in the UI.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE FIX
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When a line item has a `backgroundPhoto` (i.e., customer
selected a green-screen background), a small 28×28 inset of
the chosen background photo now sits in the bottom-right
corner of the player thumbnail. The operator immediately sees:

  ┌──────────────┐
  │              │
  │   PLAYER     │
  │              │
  │         ┌──┐ │
  │         │BG│ │
  │         └──┘ │
  └──────────────┘

Why an inset rather than a real composite preview:
  - The chroma-key (removing green) is a server-side render
    operation. Doing it client-side would mean either
    fetching/computing it on every page load (expensive) or
    adding a new endpoint with caching. Out of scope for a
    thumbnail.
  - The inset is honest: it shows both inputs to the
    composite, which is what the operator actually needs to
    verify — "right player photo + right background photo."
  - The green pixels of the player photo are still visible
    (the inset doesn't try to "fake" the composite), so the
    operator knows this is a preview of INPUTS, not a final
    render.

The inset also has its own click-through to open the full-size
background photo in a new tab (separate from the player's
click-through), and a tooltip showing the background's
filename.

Both URLs prefer fullUrl for un-watermarked. Falls back through
largeUrl → thumbUrl if fullUrl is missing.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOW TO VERIFY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  1. Open an order that has a green-screen line item with a
     selected background.
  2. The thumbnail should show the player photo (un-watermarked)
     with a small inset of the background in the bottom-right.
  3. Hover the inset — tooltip shows the background's filename.
  4. Click the inset — opens the full-size background in a new
     tab (separate click target from the player photo).
  5. Open a regular (non-green-screen) line item. Thumbnail
     looks the same as before — no inset.

FOLLOW-UP IDEAS (not in this phase)

- Actual server-rendered composite preview. Would need:
    - New endpoint that does the chroma-key + composite
    - Cache the rendered thumb so subsequent loads are fast
    - Fall back gracefully if composite fails

  before processing. The inset suffices for "which inputs"
  but not for "did the chroma-key work right" — that needs
  the real render.
