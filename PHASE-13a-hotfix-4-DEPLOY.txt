═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT WAS GOING WRONG
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Server log showed nodemon restarting but never mentioned anything
about ShipStation or appSettings — confirming the routes weren't
being registered. The "Not found" was coming from this existing
catch-all in your index.js:

    // 404 catch-all for /api/*
    app.use('/api', (req, res) => {
      res.status(404).json({ error: 'Not found', path: req.originalUrl });
    });

With shipstationRoutes mounted at /api/shipstation BEFORE that
catch-all, the new route takes priority.
