Phase 17 — Green-screen background compositing

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT THIS PHASE DOES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

For green-screen line items on SKUs that DON'T use the composite
engine (regular 8x10, 5x7, wallets, etc.), composites the chosen
photo background under the keyed-out subject. Without this, the
transparent PNG goes through imposition and prints with the page
color showing through behind the figure.

Composite engine (Memory Mate, etc.) already handles its own
backgrounds via the playerBackground slot — this phase doesn't
touch those.

Three places get the compositing:
  1. Production pipeline (processingService Step 1.6)
  2. Imposition preview endpoint (GET .../imposition/preview/...)
  3. Imposition save endpoint (POST .../imposition/preview/.../save)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT'S IN THIS TARBALL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

NEW:
  server/services/greenscreenService.js
    Singleton with composeWithBackground() and shouldComposite().
    Uses sharp, fit:'cover' for background sizing, JPEG quality 92.

MODIFIED:
  server/services/processingService.js
    Inserted Step 1.6 between Step 1.5 (composite) and Step 2
    (imposition). Iterates line items, calls greenscreenService for
    any that pass shouldComposite() and aren't already handled by
    the composite engine.

  server/routes/sytist.js
    Both imposition endpoints (preview GET + save POST) now check
    shouldComposite() and if true, fetch the subject, composite the
    background, and feed the composited buffer directly into
    impositionService.buildSheetBuffer().

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
COMPOSITING RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  - Background sized with sharp fit:'cover' — fills the entire
    canvas of the subject, aspect ratio preserved, overflow cropped
    on the long axis. No blank pixels.
  - Subject layered on top at (0,0)
  - Output JPEG at quality 92
  - On any failure (background URL missing, fetch fails, resize
    fails), falls back to subject-only and logs a warning. The
    order doesn't crash.
