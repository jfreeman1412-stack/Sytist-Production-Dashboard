    │ │                              │ │ line item    │
    │ └──────────────────────────────┘ │ picker       │
    └──────────────────────────────────┴──────────────┘

Three changes:

  1. DESIGNER FILLS LEFT COLUMN
     - Canvas size now responds to the actual rendered width of
       the left column (was: fixed 950px).
     - ResizeObserver watches the column; canvas dimensions
       recompute on resize. On a 27" monitor you get a big canvas.
     - Fallback width 1100px if measurement isn't ready yet (avoids
       a comically small initial paint).
     - Height bumped by 50% vs natural aspect fit so the canvas
       has more vertical room for bleed work.

  2. PREVIEW CONTROLS MOVED RIGHT
     - CanvasFooterToolbar (snap toggle, preview mode, Order ID,
       line items picker, error messages) now lives in the right
       column UNDER the Layers panel.
     - Nothing below the canvas anymore.
     - Right column grid changed from 3:1 to 7:3 (~30% wide).

  3. TEXT BUMPED AGAIN in SettingsForm.js (affects ALL settings
     pages):
        FormRow label:       13 → 15
        FormRow hint:        12 → 14
        baseInputStyle:      14 → 16   (TextInput, NumberInput, Select, TextArea)
        Button:              13 → 15
        Section h2:          14 → 16
        Section description: 12 → 14
        PageHeader subtitle: 13 → 15
        StatusBanner:        13 → 15
        settingsStyles.table:13 → 15
        settingsStyles.th:   11 → 13
