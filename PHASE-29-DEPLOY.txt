Phase 29 — OrderDetailPage card consolidation

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WHAT THIS DOES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Three visual cleanups to the order detail page so it doesn't take
up so much vertical space at the top:

1. MERGE Process + Production Status — what used to be two stacked
   full-width cards is now a single two-column card. Process button
   on the left; "Production Status" with Mark Shipped / Mark Back
   to Printing on the right; vertical divider between them. On
   narrow screens (<280px each side) the columns wrap to stacked.

2. DROP "(id 0)" suffix from the Production Status display.
   Currently the status reads "Open (id 0)" — Phase 29 shows just
   "Open". The status name alone is what operators care about.

3. COLLAPSE the Shipping card by default. The full card with
   carrier/service/dims form was the largest single thing on the
   page even when the operator wasn't interacting with it. Now it
   defaults to a one-line summary header. Click to expand for the
   full controls. When the order is Shipped, the collapsed header
   shows the tracking number inline so you can see it at a glance.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
COLLAPSED SHIPPING HEADER — STATES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Adapts to the four possible ShipStation states:

  SHIPPED:
    Shipping · ✓ Shipped · USPS 9400123456789012345678

  LINKED (sent, not yet shipped):
    Shipping · Sent to ShipStation · stamps_com/usps_first_class_mail

  ELIGIBLE (not yet sent, will use suggested packaging if present):
    Shipping · Eligible · 9x11 Flat Mailer · 4oz · stamps_com

  NOT ELIGIBLE:
    Shipping · Not eligible · all digital products

Click anywhere on the collapsed header OR the chevron (▾) to expand.
Click the chevron (▴) in the expanded card header to re-collapse.

NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  The collapsed state isn't persisted — it resets to collapsed on
  every navigation between orders. That's intentional: most of the
  time operators are just glancing at the status, not editing
  carrier/service. The expand action is cheap, so the default
  collapsed state matches the common case.

  If you'd prefer the Shipping card to auto-expand when the order
  is Shipped (so tracking is always fully visible), I can flip
  that easily — let me know.
